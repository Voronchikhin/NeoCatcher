# Trial and purchase

NeoCatcher Pro has a **30-day free trial**. Licenses are sold by JetBrains Marketplace, which handles payment,
invoices and taxes; you sign in with a free [JetBrains Account](https://account.jetbrains.com/).

The top right of the **Server** tab shows your edition: **Free** or **Pro**.

## Start the free trial

1. Open the license dialog in one of these ways:
    - click **Get Pro…** in the top right of the **Server** tab;
    - click **Start free trial or buy…** in the notification that appears when you use a Pro feature;
    - choose **Help → Register…** and select **NeoCatcher** in the list.
2. Sign in with your JetBrains Account and start the trial (**Start Trial** or **Evaluate for free**, depending on
   your IDE version).
3. The **Server** tab switches to **Pro**. No restart needed.

!!! tip "Android Studio"
    Android Studio has no license check of its own. It comes with the free **JetBrains Marketplace Licensing**
    plugin, which the IDE usually installs together with NeoCatcher. If **Help → Register…** doesn't list NeoCatcher,
    or a Pro notification says the license check is not available:

    1. Click **Open Plugins Settings…** in that notification (or open **Settings → Plugins** yourself), switch to
       **Marketplace** and search for *JetBrains Marketplace Licensing*.
    2. Install it and restart Android Studio.
    3. Start the trial as above.

## Buy a license

| License | Monthly | Yearly |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Personal (you pay) | $4.90 | $49 |
| Commercial (your company pays, per user) | $9.90 | $99 |

Buy in either place:

- **In the IDE:** open the license dialog as above and click **Buy license**.
- **On the web:** open the [NeoCatcher pricing page](https://plugins.jetbrains.com/plugin/35220-neocatcher/pricing)
  on JetBrains Marketplace, choose **For individual use** or **For organizations**, then **Buy**.

The license is added to the JetBrains Account you buy with, and a confirmation arrives by email.

## Activate a license you bought

1. **Help → Register…** → **NeoCatcher**.
2. Sign in with the JetBrains Account that holds the license (or that your company assigned it to), or paste an
   activation code.
3. The **Server** tab switches to **Pro**.

A company license is assigned to you by your organization's JetBrains Account administrator; then sign in as above.

## When the trial ends

Pro features stop; nothing is deleted. Your rules stay saved: the first three keep running and the others are
turned off. Breakpoints stay in the list but no longer pause responses. After you buy a license, breakpoints pause
again at once; switch the extra rules back on yourself.

A yearly subscription, or 12 monthly payments in a row, includes a perpetual fallback license: if you stop paying,
you keep Pro for the version you had.

## Free licenses

Students, teachers and maintainers of open-source projects get Pro for free: email
[support@neocatcher.com](mailto:support@neocatcher.com). JetBrains' own discounts for students, startups and
non-profits also apply, see the [pricing page](https://plugins.jetbrains.com/plugin/35220-neocatcher/pricing).
