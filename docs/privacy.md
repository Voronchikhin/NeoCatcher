*Last updated: 19 October 2026*

**Short version:** the NeoCatcher plugin does not send us your traffic or any data about how you use it.
We only receive what you send us yourself, such as support emails.

## Who we are

Maksim Voronchikhin, an individual in Belgrade, Republic of Serbia, is the controller of the personal data described
here. Contact: [privacy@neocatcher.com](mailto:privacy@neocatcher.com).

## The plugin

- Traffic captured by the plugin stays on your computer, in the IDE's memory.
- Rules and breakpoints are saved in your project and workspace files.
- The CA certificate and private key are generated and stored on your computer; keystore passwords are kept in the
  IDE's password safe.
- The plugin contains no telemetry, analytics or crash reporting of its own.

If you enable the MCP server and connect an AI agent (for example Claude Code or Cursor), the traffic the agent
reads is sent to that agent's AI provider by the agent, not by us, and is handled under that provider's terms. Secret
headers are redacted by default. Data you let any other tool read is handled by that tool's
provider under its own terms.

## Purchases

Pro licenses are sold by JetBrains s.r.o. through JetBrains Marketplace. JetBrains processes your account, payment
and license data as a separate controller under its own privacy policy. We receive sales reports from JetBrains,
which can include the buyer's company name and country.

## This website

- Hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log IP addresses for security, see GitHub's privacy statement.
- The home page shows JetBrains Marketplace's install button, loaded from plugins.jetbrains.com. Your browser
  then connects to JetBrains, which receives your IP address and sets technical load-balancing cookies
  (AWSALB, AWSALBCORS) under the [JetBrains privacy policy](https://www.jetbrains.com/legal/docs/privacy/privacy/).
- Our own pages use no analytics and set no cookies.

## Support email

When you email us we process your address and message to answer you, and keep them for up to 24 months after
the conversation ends. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in providing support, or steps before/under a contract.

## Your rights

Under the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection and, where it applies, the EU GDPR, you can ask for access,
correction, deletion, restriction, portability and object to processing. You can also complain to the Commissioner
for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection of Serbia (poverenik.rs) or your local
supervisory authority.

## Changes

We will update this notice when our practices change; the date at the top shows the latest version.
