## The plugin

- Traffic captured by the plugin stays on your computer, in the IDE's memory.
- Rules and breakpoints are saved in your project and workspace files.
- The CA certificate and private key are generated and stored on your computer; keystore passwords are kept in the
  IDE's password safe.
- The plugin contains no telemetry, analytics or crash reporting of its own.

If you enable the MCP server and connect an AI agent (for example Claude Code or Cursor), the traffic the agent
reads is sent to that agent's AI provider by the agent, not by us, and is handled under that provider's terms. Secret
headers are redacted by default. Data you let any other tool read is handled by that tool's
provider under its own terms.
